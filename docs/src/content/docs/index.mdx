The public studies made with the max-models codes: verification of the codes, demonstrations of
the methods and examples. Each study is a complete, standalone script with its result cached,
and its status box says what it verifies and what it does not.

	<LinkCard title="Examples" href="/gallery/by-kind/example/" description="Uses of the codes and estimates." />

The codes are the max-models packages ([github.com/max-models](https://github.com/max-models)),
and the site is built with [gallery-tools](https://github.com/spokes3d/gallery-template).
